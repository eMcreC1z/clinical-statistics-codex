# Journal Statistical Review

## Review Workflow

1. Identify study design and reporting guideline: CONSORT, STROBE, STARD, TRIPOD, RECORD, or PRISMA.
2. Check whether the objective, population, endpoint, exposure or intervention, comparator, and time window are clear.
3. Review statistical methods for endpoint scale, model assumptions, covariate handling, missing data, and multiplicity.
4. Compare results text against tables and figures for consistency.
5. Prioritize major validity issues before style edits.

## Common Findings

- Missing analysis population definition.
- Incomplete endpoint derivation.
- P values reported without effect estimates and confidence intervals.
- Unclear missing-data assumptions.
- Uncontrolled multiplicity for confirmatory claims.
- Overinterpretation of subgroup or exploratory analyses.
- Prediction models lacking calibration or validation.
