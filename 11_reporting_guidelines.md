# 11 Reporting Guidelines

## Common Guidelines

- CONSORT: randomized trials.
- STROBE: observational studies.
- STARD: diagnostic accuracy studies.
- TRIPOD: prediction model development and validation.
- RECORD: studies using routinely collected health data.
- PRISMA: systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

## Statistical Methods Section

Include:

- Analysis population.
- Endpoint definitions.
- Descriptive statistics.
- Primary model and covariates.
- Missing data handling.
- Multiplicity control.
- Sensitivity and subgroup analyses.
- Software and version.

## Results Section

Include:

- Participant flow and exclusions.
- Baseline characteristics.
- Follow-up and missing data.
- Primary and secondary endpoint results.
- Effect estimates with confidence intervals.
- Model diagnostics or assumption checks when relevant.

## Reviewer Response

For statistical reviewer comments, respond with:

- A direct answer.
- The change made in the manuscript or supplement.
- A method rationale.
- Added sensitivity analysis if needed.
- Exact page, line, table, or figure reference.
