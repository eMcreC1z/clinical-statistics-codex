# 09 Diagnostic Prediction

## Diagnostic Accuracy

- Define target condition, reference standard, index test, threshold, and intended-use population.
- Report sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, likelihood ratios, and uncertainty intervals.
- Consider prevalence when interpreting predictive values.

## Prediction Modeling

- Separate development, internal validation, and external validation.
- Pre-specify candidate predictors and outcome time horizon.
- Avoid leakage from future data or outcome-derived variables.
- Use penalization or shrinkage when overfitting risk is high.

## Model Performance

- Discrimination: C statistic or AUC.
- Calibration: calibration-in-the-large, calibration slope, calibration plot.
- Clinical usefulness: decision curve analysis when appropriate.
- Classification: threshold-specific sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, and net benefit.

## Reporting

Use TRIPOD or TRIPOD-AI as applicable. Report data source, missing data handling, sample size, events, predictor coding, validation method, and final model specification.
